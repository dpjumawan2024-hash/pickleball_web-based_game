Rules

  • ScoringFirst to 11, win by 2. Fair rally mode scores every rally. In official side-out mode only the server scores; if the server loses, the serve changes with no point.
  • ServeServe underhand and cross-court into the opposite service box, past the kitchen. Singles allows one serve attempt per turn. Service-box lines are in.
  • Two-bounceThe serve and return must bounce before either player can volley. Return the ball before its second bounce.
  • KitchenNo volley while touching the kitchen or its line. You may enter to play a ball after it bounces.
  • In / OutKeep the ball in bounds; lines are in. A ball hit out, into the net, or not returned in time loses the rally.
  • Score callCall the server's score first.
